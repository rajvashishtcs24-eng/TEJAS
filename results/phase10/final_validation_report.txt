5. SCOPE, LIMITATIONS & FUTURE EXTENSIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Advisory Decision Support Only: System provides recommendations to operating
   engineers; it does NOT connect to SCADA/PLC for closed-loop automatic control.
2. Quasi-Static Lumped Physics: Inertia is modeled via SHM kinematics; elastic stress
   wave reflections (Gibbs wave equation) are not solved in real-time.
3. No Real-World Field Calibration Claimed: All models are calibrated on synthetic
   and physics-approximated domain equations.
4. Target / Future Extensions:
   - Full 1D damped wave equation downhole card diagnostic reconstruction (Everitt-Jennings).
   - Multi-phase reservoir inflow transient response integration.
   - SCADA OPC-UA / MQTT live industrial telemetry ingestion.
