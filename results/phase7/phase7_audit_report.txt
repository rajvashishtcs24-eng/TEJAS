2. AUDIT OF QUESTIONABLE RELATIONSHIPS & PHYSICAL CALIBRATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Issue 1: Thermal Heating (+35 °F) -> Viscosity dropped (-531 cP) but delta_Production was 0.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Diagnosis: Previously, theoretical pump displacement was evaluated assuming 100% volumetric
  efficiency whenever fluid level was above pump. In cold heavy crude (850 cP), two real physics
  mechanisms suppress baseline volumetric delivery:
    a) Viscous standing valve intake throttling lag: eta_valve(mu)
    b) Downstroke rod float kinematics lag: eta_rf_stroke(Risk_RF)
- Resolution in Phase 7.5:
  Coupled volumetric delivery: eta_vol = eta_fill * eta_valve(mu) * eta_rf_stroke(Risk_RF).
  At 850 cP and high rod float, baseline delivery efficiency is ~72%.
  Thermal CSS heating (+35 °F, 319 cP) eliminates rod float and valve choking, raising eta_vol to 100%,
  producing a physically authentic production recovery (+80.9 bpd) without artificial forcing.

Issue 2: SPM Reduction -> Severe linear production drop.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Diagnosis: Pure kinematic scaling (Q ~ SPM) overstates production loss when reducing speed
  on a heavily floating or pounding well.
- Resolution in Phase 7.5:
  Throttling SPM from 8.5 to 5.0 reduces theoretical displacement, but volumetric delivery efficiency
  improves from 71.9% to 99.8%. The net production loss is cushioned (-119.3 bpd instead of -177.6 bpd),
  while cutting polished rod power demand by -6.40 kW.

Issue 3: POC / Annular Fluid-Level Recovery -> Positive production response.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Diagnosis: Slowing down pump speed on an inflow-depleted well increased net production (+39.2 bpd).
- Physical Validity: FULLY SOUND (Classic Pump-Off Control principle). Pumping at 7.5 SPM on an
  inflow-depleted well yields 50% barrel fillage (pounding). Throttling to 4.8 SPM allows fluid level
  to recharge, raising barrel fillage to 93%, delivering higher net liquid with lower power and zero shock.

Issue 4: Aggressive Overpumping -> Marginal production gain at extreme power penalty.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Diagnosis: Forcing SPM from 5.5 to 10.5 draws down fluid level, giving only +2.1 bpd net liquid
  while power jumps +5.74 kW (+178%) and fluid pound risk jumps into High tier (73.4/100).
- Physical Validity: FULLY SOUND. Demonstrates the steep diminishing returns of uncoupled drawdown
  and catastrophic pump-off.
